Meet Xavi
A rocket-powered block cadet who flies every mission. Answer right and the boosters go full burn — a fast hit does double damage and sends whole walls down.

How it works

  1. A math problem shows up — pick the correct answer out of four.
  2. Right answer = full-power boosters. Wrong answer = weaker shot, but you still get to fire.
  3. Pull Xavi back, then let go — he rockets toward the building.
  4. Knock out the pillars and the floors above come crashing down.
