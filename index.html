How it works

  1. A math problem shows up — pick the right answer out of four.
  2. Right answer: pull Xavi back and let go to launch him at the building.
  3. Wrong answer: the minion fires a bonk-missile and Xavi loses a power bar. Three bonks and he's out!
  4. Three right in a row (or flying through a golden Star Cell) gives a super shot.
  5. Knock the building down to beat the minion. Beat all ten to save Feyre!
